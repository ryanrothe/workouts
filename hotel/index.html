How this tracker works

Two workouts, both from John Madsen. Tap Upper Body or Legs to switch between them. Within each, tap the + New pill to start a new dated session, check off exercises as you finish them, and tap ✎ Log sets to record every set.

Each set logs reps and weight (added weight on pull-ups and leg raises). The = 3 × 15 button fills the targets in one tap; type over what was different. Pull-up waves log reps per wave (target 10: 2 + 3 + 5).

The Last: line under each exercise shows the sets from your previous session ("15, 15, 12 @ 25 lb · short"), and a chip suggests the load: every set at the target adds 5 lb, falling short holds the weight, three short sessions at one weight drop it ~10%. Max-rep work says Beat last.

Each workout header shows a planned time and every exercise a "start ~N min in" marker. Tap ▶ Clock in the session toolbar when you start — it survives reloads and reports pace against the plan once you check off your first exercise. The ⏱ 60-min mode toggle tightens every rest one step (90s→60s, 60s→45s) and recomputes the plan and the rest timers; sets and reps stay as written.

Key technique cues

  • Pull-up waves (2/3/5): 2 reps, short rest, 3 reps, short rest, 5 reps — that's 1 wave. Rest 60–90s, then next wave. Total: 70 reps across 21 mini-sets.
  • Goblet squat tempo: 6 sec down, 6 sec pause at bottom, drive up at normal speed. Each rep ~13–15 sec. Pick a weight you can hold through all 50 reps with form intact — usually lighter than you'd guess.
  • Lateral / side raise superset: Lateral raise straight into side raise without rest, then rest before the next round.
  • If weights are too light: Swap in single-leg variations (single-leg goblet, single-leg RDL). Bulgarians are already unilateral.
